# Vincula — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Pipeline lead at the national AI centre

- As a pipeline lead, I want each project held at a named stage with that stage's evidence attached, so that a prototype cannot be described as a service and a publication cannot be described as an invention.
- As a pipeline lead, I want cohort conversion measured against the ratios we declared when we opened the portfolio, so that fifty proofs of concept producing no running service is a visible failure rather than a busy year.
- As a pipeline lead, I want to see the share of our award money that went to propositions which had already succeeded commercially, so that a fund created to take risk can be held to it.
- As a pipeline lead, I want state attribution and a concentration measure on every national figure, so that our reporting does not describe the capital and call it the country.

### University technology transfer officer

- As a transfer officer, I want to attempt to route a software-only invention to a patent filing and have the system refuse it and offer the author's rights route instead, so that we do not spend a year and a budget on an application the law cannot grant.
- As a transfer officer, I want inventions we could not protect at all recorded with the reason, so that the reform case is built from our own file rather than from anecdote.
- As a transfer officer, I want to record commercial validation and operation manuals against a prototype, so that a working demonstration is distinguishable from something a company could actually adopt.
- As a transfer officer, I want the equity the public fund holds in our spin-out visible alongside the founders' declared joint appointments, so that a conflict question can be answered from the record.

### Research council programme officer

- As a programme officer, I want awards linked to the sector council priority they were made under and the share of the programme allocated that way, so that industry priority-setting is auditable rather than ceremonial.
- As a programme officer, I want to grade the novelty and risk of an award at the point of decision, so that the portfolio's risk profile is a recorded choice rather than a retrospective description.
- As a programme officer, I want a project's award record to carry forward into the invention and prototype stages, so that the money we spent can be connected to what eventually reached the market.

### Postgraduate programme and talent lead

- As a talent lead, I want programmes recorded with a maturity tier and an international-standard assessment, so that a count of programmes is not mistaken for a count of capacity.
- As a talent lead, I want student cohorts followed to first destination with returns from abroad reported apart from domestic recruitment, so that we know whether the graduates we funded went into domestic industry, domestic academia, the public sector or abroad, and so that an attraction campaign is not credited with hires it did not cause.
- As a talent lead, I want researchers' formal classification compared with their actual field and mismatches counted, so that the case for a dedicated computational sciences board rests on numbers.

### Transition and governance administrator

- As a transition administrator, I want a handover pack listing every live commitment with its stage, money and dependencies, so that an incoming administration inherits a record rather than a rumour.
- As a governance administrator, I want each commitment explicitly re-adopted, modified or dropped within the handover window and anything unaddressed flagged as orphaned, so that continuity is a decision someone made rather than an accident of who kept their office.
